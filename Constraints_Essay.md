# Professional
Our ultimate goal is to as professional of a project as we can. This means that our group will have to make the scop of the project reasonable so the quality of the final product is good. Since we have two people over a 2 semester time frame, the scope of this project should be limited. 
# Social
This project is inheretly 'fun' and something that anybody could enjoy. This means that we will need to design a product with accessability features and something that end users will feel satisfied purchasing. This means that the product will have to satisfy a variety of customer needs that will be addressed later.
# Economic
Becuase we have no current support, the cost of hardware or software will be handled by the team members. This is going to mean that we will not be spending spurratically and cutting costs will have to be considered for each potential purchase. The lower budget will put pressure on the social and professional aspects of this product, and will have to be actively maintained/monitored.
